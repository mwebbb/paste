How it works

Pastes are encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before they are uploaded. The key is put in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to the server. The server stores only ciphertext and cannot read your paste.

This means if you lose the link, the paste is gone. Nobody can recover it.

What is stored

The encrypted paste, its size, and when it was created. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies for visitors. Standard hosting logs may record requests.

Rules

  • Don't use this for anything illegal.
  • Don't use it to host malware or to harass people.
  • Pastes are limited to 256 KB.

Removal

Any paste can be deleted at any time, for any reason, without notice. If you send a full link reporting abuse, it may be opened and removed.
